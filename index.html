❓ How to Play
🃏
Flip the card — tap it (or press Space) to reveal the definition, synonym, and antonym on the back.
✅
Judge yourself honestly — after flipping, pick Again, Hard, or Easy based on how well you recalled the word.
🎯
Focus Mode — only shows cards you marked Again or Hard. Use it to drill your weak spots.
📈
Mastery bar — fills up as you mark cards Easy. Try to get it to 100%!
🔊
Pronunciation — tap the 🔊 button on any card to hear the word spoken aloud.
